# billing (`hospital-billing`)

Billing at the front desk ([docs/BILLING_PLAN.md](../../docs/BILLING_PLAN.md)): a consultation fee per doctor, one bill per visit (made at check-in), payments and refunds recorded at the desk, PDF receipts. Patients see their own bills and download the receipts ([docs/PATIENT_BILLS_PLAN.md](../../docs/PATIENT_BILLS_PLAN.md)), and pay them online with Razorpay ([docs/ONLINE_PAYMENT_PLAN.md](../../docs/ONLINE_PAYMENT_PLAN.md)).

**Depends on:** common, identity, patients, staff, appointments · **Used by:** app

## Public API — `com.itmonteur.hospitalerp.billing`

None: no other module uses billing. It learns about visits from the appointments module's events.

## Events

- **Listens to** (appointments; `BillingEventListener`, plain `@EventListener`s, so inside the appointment's transaction — a failed check-in leaves no bill, and a bill that can't be made fails the check-in):
  - `AppointmentCheckedInEvent` → `BillService.openBill`: the visit's bill (`B-2026-000123`, numbered from its id right after the insert) with one fixed line, "Consultation — Dr. …" at the doctor's fee (`FeeService.feeFor`, else the default); a cancelled bill is reopened, a paid one stays paid. One bill per appointment.
  - `AppointmentLeftQueueEvent` (check-in undone, or rescheduled) → `leftQueue`: an unpaid bill is cancelled.
  - `AppointmentCancelledEvent` (by the patient or the front desk, a doctor's approved leave, an account deactivation) → `visitCancelled`: unpaid → cancelled, paid → refund due.

## Endpoints — `billing.web`

| Controller | URL |
|---|---|
| `FeeController` | `GET /api/admin/fees` — every doctor (deactivated ones too, `active: false`) with `consultationFee` (null when none is set), by name, and `defaultFee`; `PUT /api/admin/fees/{doctorId}` `{"consultationFee": 500}` — 0 to 1,00,000 rupees, at most 2 decimals (400 otherwise, 404 for an unknown doctor). Admins only (`/api/admin/**`, administration's access rules). |
| `BillController` | `/api/receptionist/bills` — receptionists and admins (staff's access rules): `GET` (newest first; `from`, `to`, `status` = unpaid / paid / refund_due / refunded / cancelled / all, `patient` = part of the name or a bill number; a `PageResult` of `BillSummaryDTO`, 20 per page, at most 100), `GET /{id}` and `GET /by-appointment/{appointmentId}` (`BillDTO`; 404 before check-in), `POST /{id}/items` `{description, amount}` and `DELETE /{id}/items/{itemId}` (unpaid only; not the consultation line), `PUT /{id}/discount` `{discount}` (unpaid only; 0 up to the subtotal), `POST /{id}/pay` `{method: CASH / CARD / UPI, reference?}` (unpaid only), `POST /{id}/refund` `{method, reference?}` (refund due only), `POST /{id}/refund-online` (a cancelled visit paid online: refunded through Razorpay, recorded with method `ONLINE`, the refund id, who and when; 409 for a bill paid at the desk or not due for refund, 502 when Razorpay refuses), `GET /{id}/receipt.pdf` (paid, refund due or refunded). `BillDTO.unappliedPayments`: online payments that came when the bill was already paid, changed or cancelled — refunding, refunded, or refund failed (to refund by hand in the Razorpay dashboard). A change to a bill in the wrong state → 409; wrong values → 400. |
| `DoctorEarningsController` | A doctor's earnings ([docs/DOCTOR_ANALYTICS_PLAN.md](../../docs/DOCTOR_ANALYTICS_PLAN.md)): `GET /api/doctor/earnings?days=` (the logged-in doctor's; staff's `/api/doctor/**` rules: doctors and admins; 404 without a doctor profile) and `GET /api/admin/doctors/{doctorId}/earnings?days=` (admins); 7, 30 or 90 days ending today (400 otherwise), 30 by default. `DoctorEarningsDTO`: earned (paid, not refunded) also per day by visit date, paid visits, the average per paid visit (null without any), refunds (refund due or refunded) and unpaid, each with its number of visits; cancelled bills left out. |
| `CashSummaryController` | The daily cash summary ([docs/DAILY_CASH_SUMMARY_PLAN.md](../../docs/DAILY_CASH_SUMMARY_PLAN.md)): `GET /api/receptionist/cash-summary` (today; receptionists and admins, staff's rules), `GET /api/admin/cash-summary?date=` (any day up to today) and `GET /api/admin/cash-summary/range?from=&to=` (both included, at most 31 days, not after today; admins). `CashSummaryDTO`: per method (`CASH`, `CARD`, `UPI`, `ONLINE`, always all four) the payments, refunds and net; the totals; per person who took the money (`by`; null: paid online by the patient), by name with the online payments last; the lines (time, `PAYMENT` / `REFUND`, bill, patient, doctor, method, reference, by whom, amount) in the order they happened; and `unpaid`, the day's visits whose bills are still unpaid. `CashRangeDTO`: a row per day (every day; the totals and `cashNet`, the cash's own net), the methods and the totals of the range. Wrong days → 400. |
| `RazorpayWebhookController` | `POST /api/payments/razorpay/webhook` — open to everyone (`BillingSecurityRules`); the `X-Razorpay-Signature` (HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body with `RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET`) decides: 400 when wrong or missing. `payment.captured` / `order.paid` for one of our orders is applied like the app's confirmation (any number of times); other events and orders are ignored (200); 503 when Razorpay can't be reached to check the payment, so Razorpay sends it again. |
| `MyBillController` | `/api/bills/my` — patients only (`BillingSecurityRules`): `GET` (the patient's bills — their own visits' and their family members' — newest first, each with its lines, amounts, payment and refund as `MyBillDTO`; cancelled bills left out), `GET /{id}/receipt.pdf` (paid, refund due or refunded; 409 before payment; 404 for another patient's or a cancelled bill). Online payment (each bill has `payOnline`: Razorpay is set up, the bill is unpaid and at least Rs. 1): `POST /{id}/online-payment` (the Razorpay order for Checkout — key id, order id, amount in paise, currency, name, description, the patient's details; an unused order for the same total is reused; 409 when it can't be paid online, 502 when Razorpay can't be reached), `POST /{id}/online-payment/confirm` `{orderId, paymentId, signature}` (Checkout's answer: 400 for a wrong signature; the paid bill; 409 with the reason when the payment couldn't be applied and was refunded). |

## Internal — `billing.internal`

`BillService`, `BillingEventListener` (above); `BillDeskService` (the front desk's list, bill, items, discount, payment, refund, receipt — who took or gave back the money is kept on the bill; `BillDTO`, `BillSummaryDTO`); `MyBillService` (the logged-in patient's bills, by the account's patient id, which a bill keeps for family members' visits too; `MyBillDTO`: the desk's view without the staff user names, the patient id and the item ids; the receipt through `BillDeskService.receiptOf`, the desk's rule); `BillingSecurityRules` (`/api/bills/my/**` for patients); `OnlinePaymentService` (the order, the confirmation, the webhook, the desk's online refund, and applying a payment — repeatable, the bill locked; an authorized payment is captured; a payment that can't be applied any more because the bill was paid, changed or cancelled meanwhile is refunded at once), `RazorpayClient` (Razorpay's REST API with Spring's `RestClient`: orders, fetch / capture / refund a payment; signatures checked by `RazorpaySignatures`, HMAC-SHA256), `OnlinePayment` / `OnlinePaymentRepository` / `OnlinePaymentStatus` (one row per Razorpay order: created → paid, or refunding → refunded / refund failed); the desk's changes to a bill lock it (`BillRepository.findByIdForUpdate`), and the desk can't record a payment as `ONLINE`; `CashSummaryService` (the daily cash summary from the bills paid or refunded in a period — `BillRepository.findPaidOrRefundedBetween`, with their lines: a payment counts on the day it was made whatever happened to the bill later, a refund on the day it was given, always the whole bill; amounts are the bills' totals), `CashSummaryDTO`, `CashRangeDTO`; `DoctorEarnings` (a doctor's earnings from two grouped queries — the bills' discounts and their lines per visit date and status — with the appointments module's `DoctorAnalytics.PERIODS`), `DoctorEarningsDTO`; `ReceiptPdfService` (A4 receipt with the `HOSPITAL_*` letterhead: lines, subtotal, discount, total, the payment and the refund; amounts as "Rs. 1,250.00" — the PDF's standard fonts have no rupee sign); `FeeService` (`listFees`, `setFee` — records when and by whom; `feeFor(doctorId)`: the doctor's fee or the default; `defaultFee()`), `DoctorFee` / `DoctorFeeRepository`, `DoctorFeeDTO`, `FeeListDTO`; `Bill` (one per appointment: number, patient, doctor, visit date, `BillStatus`, discount, items, payment and refund details; `subtotal()`, `total()`), `BillItem` (description, amount, `consultation` = the doctor's fee line), `BillStatus` (`UNPAID`, `PAID`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUND_DUE`, `REFUNDED`), `PaymentMethod` (`CASH`, `CARD`, `UPI`; `ONLINE` for Razorpay), `BillRepository` (also a `JpaSpecificationExecutor` for the list).

## Database

Flyway `billing/V2026_10_12_1__billing.sql`: `doctor_fees` (one row per doctor with a fee), `bills` (unique per `appointment_id` and per `bill_number`; indexed by status and visit date), `bill_items` (deleted with their bill). No foreign keys to appointments, patients or doctors: bills are financial records and keep the names they were made with. Amounts are `decimal(10,2)` rupees.

Flyway `billing/V2026_10_15_1__online_payments.sql`: `online_payments` (one row per Razorpay order: the bill, the order id — unique —, the amount, the status, the payment and refund ids, why it was refunded, the times; a foreign key to `bills`).

## Configuration

| Env variable | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `BILLING_DEFAULT_FEE` | `0` | Consultation fee in rupees for doctors without their own. |
| `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID`, `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET` | empty | Razorpay's API keys (test keys start with `rzp_test_`); online payment is on only when both are set. |
| `RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET` | empty | The secret of the Razorpay webhook; without it every webhook is refused. |
| `RAZORPAY_API_URL` | `https://api.razorpay.com/v1` | For tests only. |
